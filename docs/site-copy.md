## What I can rebuild

If your team uses a small part of a big tool, that part is usually a good candidate.

**Good to rebuild**
- Form builders like Jotform and Typeform
- Approval and expense flows
- Volunteer rotas and sign-up sheets
- Booking and scheduling
- Trackers, contact lists and simple CRMs
- Internal dashboards and reports

**Keep paying for**
- Accounting software
- Payroll
- Gift Aid and HMRC submissions

These subscriptions keep you compliant as the rules change. They're worth the money, and I'll tell you so.

## How it works

1. **Tell me what you pay for.** Send me the subscription and what your team uses it for. I'll tell you straight if a rebuild is worth it.
2. **Get a fixed quote.** One price, agreed before I start, with a delivery date. No hourly billing.
3. **Try it with your team.** You test it with the people who'll use it before you switch the old tool off.
4. **Take ownership.** The code and hosting sit in your charity's own accounts, with handover notes written for non-technical staff.
5. **Three months of support, included.** After that, support is optional, and it costs less than the subscription did.

## Questions charities ask

**What does it cost to run?**
Often nothing. Small tools fit on free hosting plans. Busier ones can cost around £10 a month, paid straight to the host.

**What if something breaks?**
Fixes are on me for the first three months. After that you can book a fix when you need one, or take an optional support plan.

**What if you disappear?**
You own everything. The code, the hosting and the data. Any developer can pick it up from the handover notes.

**Is our data safe?**
Access is set by role, every action is logged, and sensitive data can delete itself on a schedule. Security is enforced in the database, not trusted to the browser.
