Case 01

Payment approvals, replacing paper and WhatsApp

A finance team was approving payments over WhatsApp and paper. Requests got lost, nobody could say who had authorised what, and bank details were sitting in chat histories.

I built a submission and approval system to replace it.

  • Anyone can submit a request through a public link, with no account needed
  • Receipts, e-signature, and an automatic reference number
  • The whole team is notified in real time and can approve from their phone without opening the app
  • Every action is logged, so there is a complete record of who decided what and when
  • Bank details are visible only to authorised roles and delete themselves 90 days after payment
  • Role-based access, with guardrails like preventing removal of the last admin

Built with React, TypeScript, Supabase and Postgres. Security enforced at the database level rather than trusted to the browser.
