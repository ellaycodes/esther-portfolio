    Questions charities ask

    What does it cost to run?
    Often nothing. Small tools fit on free hosting plans. Busier ones can cost around £10 a month, paid straight to the host.
    What if something breaks?
    Fixes are on me for the first three months. After that you can book a fix when you need one, or take an optional support plan.
    What if you disappear?
    You own everything. The code, the hosting and the data. Any developer can pick it up from the handover notes.
    Is our data safe?
    Access is set by role, every action is logged, and sensitive data can delete itself on a schedule. Security is enforced in the database, not trusted to the browser.
